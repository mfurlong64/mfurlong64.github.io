Recommendations

1. Fix the rules source

Priority: high. Move `TILE_DEFS`, move generation, and legality into one shared JS module, then import it into both the browser and simulator. Right now, balancing from sim output is a little haunted.

Success check: browser and sim produce identical legal moves for seeded board states.

2. Keep anti-repetition

Priority: high. Anti-repetition removed all 59 baseline repetition draws without creating obvious first-player bias.

Rule feel: no player loses agency; the AI just stops choosing stale loops.

3. Rework mobility scoring

Priority: medium. The mobility modes made games shorter and fast losses slightly worse. Reduce the weight from 4 to 1-2, or only apply it to placements adjacent to the Prince.

Goal: prevent self-boxing without making the AI over-prioritize tempo traps.

4. Split Pawn metrics

Priority: medium. Separate starting Pawns from placed Pawns. Two Pawns begin on the board, so current Pawn stats mix opening-shield behavior with normal bag-entry behavior.

Decision unlocked: whether Pawns are too strong, or the setup is doing the work.

5. Buff or clarify Mage

Priority: provisional. If the post-fix sim still shows Mage at low survival and low value efficiency, give it one survivability or reach improvement.

Small options: make one side a true jump, add one adjacent escape, or reduce its capture value expectation.

6. Rename flag wins

Priority: low. Direct flag capture is not happening, but flag cornering is. Treat this as a legitimate win path in UI and reports, not a second-class stalemate.

Suggested language: "Flag locked" or "Flag cornered".
